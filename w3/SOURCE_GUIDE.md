## Commerce

Guo & Li (2022), *Exploring the Relationship Between Social Commerce Features and Consumers' Repurchase Intentions: The Mediating Role of Perceived Value.*
[Publisher article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.775056/full).
514 valid WeChat-shopper questionnaires, cross-sectional self-report.
Table 4: five off-diagonal correlations with repurchase intention. Diagonal values are square roots of AVE, not the plotted correlations.
Figure 2: standardized beta paths .485 (utilitarian) and .284 (hedonic), both p < .001.
Publication is 2022 despite 2021 in the DOI. Collection dates were not reported in the inspected article.

## Social connections

Zhang & Jung (2023), *Time counts? A two-wave panel study investigating the effects of WeChat affordances on social capital and well-being.*
[Publisher article](https://academic.oup.com/jcmc/article/28/1/zmac030/6827860).
China, January–May 2020, two waves three months apart. 1202 valid first-wave responses and 740 second-wave responses.
Use the Structural model section and Figure 2. The prose supplies four nonsignificant paths omitted visually in the original figure. All 8 focal paths are retained in our redraw.
Standardized beta is not a percentage change. The 2023 issue follows online publication in November 2022.

## Fatigue and lurking

Liu, Feng, Chen & Yuan (2024), *“Left on read” examining social media users' lurking behavior: an integration of anxiety and social media fatigue.*
[Publisher article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1406895/full).
836 valid Chinese WeChat-user surveys, January–February 2024.
Section 5.3 / Figure 2: ten pressure-to-fatigue/anxiety paths plus two paths to lurking; all displayed p < .01.
Lurking is observing content without active participation.
Table 1: 309 / 374 / 105 / 48 respondents in <2h / 2–4h / 4–6h / >6h groups.
Retain the original labels. These are sample percentages. Do not substitute mediation estimates from another table.
